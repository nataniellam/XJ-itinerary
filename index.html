View on Google Maps ↗

Map: a live Google Map — pan, zoom and switch to satellite view same as any embedded Google Map. Each day's route is the actual driving directions between its stops, not a straight line; muted lines behind it are every other day, for context. Click a pin or a card to select its day — the matching pin turns teal instead of popping up a label.

Detours: the gold diamond and dashed line show where a detour sits relative to that day's route; selecting a day zooms the map to fit that day's stops plus the detour, so you can judge the real distance at a glance. Added time is a rough estimate, not a fixed number.

Weather: figures are October seasonal averages for each area (high / low, typical conditions), not a live forecast for your travel dates. Recheck closer to departure, especially for Kanas, Hemu, Baihaba and Sayram Lake, where an early cold snap or first snowfall can shift things fast.

Photos: freely licensed images from Wikimedia Commons, credited under each one. A handful of stops and detours (Hemu Bridge, Baihaba, Dushanzi, Jinghe, Shanshan, and a few of the smaller detours) don't have a suitable photo yet — their cards just skip the image rather than show a wrong or broken one.
